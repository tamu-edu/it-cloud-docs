# Backstage Replacing Kion

Technology Services is launching a new portal for cloud account holders: **Backstage**. Backstage is a self-service platform that gives account owners and operators financial, organizational, and security visibility into their cloud environments, access to their cloud resources, along with new capabilities that Kion did not offer.

Backstage is planned to launch on **December 1, 2026**, and Kion will be retired on **January 1, 2027**.

> [!NOTE]
> More detailed Backstage documentation will be published on this site between now and December 1. A notice with a link to Backstage will be sent when the platform is available.

## Timeline

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **December 1, 2026** | Backstage launches. Kion stops receiving new cloud cost data. All other Kion features continue to function. |
| **January 1, 2027**| Kion will be shut down, and all use transition to Backstage. |

## What Backstage Provides

Backstage is your home base for everything in the cloud: see what you're spending, know your security and compliance status, and request new resources when you need them, all in one self-service portal. Backstage connects to best-in-class tools so you get the best view of your environments.

- **Know where your money is going.** Costs and budget insights are powered by [Vantage](https://www.vantage.sh/), so you can see what you're spending, spot trends, and avoid surprises.
- **Stay secure with confidence.** Security and compliance visibility is powered by [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/), giving you a clear picture of how your accounts are doing and what needs attention.
- **See the big picture.** Understand how your projects, environments, and workspaces fit together and who is responsible for them.
- **Get what you need, when you need it.** Request and provision new cloud resources yourself, right from the portal.

Backstage is also built to grow. Technology Services and the Enterprise Compute Environment will be able to:

- Integrate with more of the tools and providers you already use, including AI agents and other agentic and automation tools.
- Make services available to request beyond the major public cloud providers.
- Offer one-click deployments of blueprints and pre-built solutions for common cloud use cases into your environments.
- Build features around the university's own processes, such as the annual assessment, attestation and reporting requirements, and the Information Resource Responsibility Agreement (IRRA).

## Getting Started with Backstage

**Your existing Kion Projects and cloud accounts will be imported into Backstage. No action is required to migrate them.**

When Backstage launches on December 1:

1. Open Backstage using the link in the launch notice.
2. Log in with your Texas A&M University NetID.
3. Find your project:
    - **Business customers**: your Kion Projects are now named **Environments**.
    - **Research customers**: your Kion Projects are now named **Workspaces**.
4. Explore information about your resources using the integrations.
5. Test accessing your accounts in AWS, Azure, and GCP.

## What Happens to Kion Data

Kion and Backstage will overlap for a short period. Use the table below to plan around what is available where.

| Data or feature | In Kion | In Backstage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud spend before December 1, 2026 | Available until January 1, 2027 | Available at launch (limited to Sept 1, 2024) |
| Cloud spend after December 1, 2026 | Not available | Available |
| Cloud Account access | Available until January 1, 2027 | Available on December 1 |
| Compliance and security findings | Available until January 1, 2027 | Available on December 1 |

Kion data will not be available after it is shut down on January 1, 2027. If you rely on any data or features that are not covered by  Backstage, please get in touch before then.

## Preparing for the Transition

- **Do a quick orientation**. Make sure you can log in with your NetID, and that you know where to find and can see everything that you need. Some Kion Projects may undergo small name changes or be merged with others when they're imported into Backstage.
- **Confirm your access.** Use the Access Profiles to confirm that you can still access your Cloud accounts. If you have a team, make sure they have also confirmed their access.
- **Plan for the January 1 cutover.** After January 1, 2027, Kion can no longer be used to access your cloud accounts. Check this site before then for how to access your accounts through Backstage.
- **Update your tooling.** Scripts, automation, or documentation that depend on Kion (including the Kion CLI) will stop working when Kion is shut down. Replacement details will be published.
- **Save what you need.** Export any Kion reports you need to keep.

> [!NOTE]
> Some Kion Projects may undergo small name changes or be merged with others when they're imported into Backstage.
