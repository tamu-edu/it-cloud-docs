# Azure for Students

[Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students/) helps eligible students access Azure credit and selected services for learning, coursework, and personal projects. This article explains how to enroll while keeping the subscription owned by a personal Microsoft account: Microsoft uses your TAMU school email address to verify your academic status, but that email does not own the subscription or place it in the TAMU Azure tenant.

## Use a personal Microsoft account

Sign up with a personal Microsoft account that uses a personal email address, such as Gmail or Yahoo. Do not use your TAMU NetID to create or own the subscription.

Microsoft will still require you to verify that you are an eligible student using your TAMU school email address. These are two separate steps:

- **Personal Microsoft account:** Owns the Azure subscription and is used to sign in to Azure.
- **TAMU school email address:** Verifies your academic status for the Azure for Students benefit.

For more information about account types, see [What's the difference between a Microsoft account and a work or school account?](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/what-s-the-difference-between-a-microsoft-account-and-a-work-or-school-account).

If you need a personal Microsoft account, create one at [https://signup.live.com](https://signup.live.com).

## Sign up for Azure for Students

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A personal Microsoft account. Create one at [https://signup.live.com](https://signup.live.com) if needed.
- An active TAMU school email address that Microsoft can use to verify your student status.
- Access to your TAMU email so that you can complete the verification process if required.

To enroll:

1. Go to the [Azure for Students offer](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students/).
2. Select **Start free** (or the equivalent enrollment button).
3. Sign in with your personal Microsoft account. If the browser is already signed in with your TAMU NetID, sign out first or use a private browsing window to avoid selecting the wrong account.
4. If Microsoft asks you to verify your academic status, enter your TAMU school email address and complete the verification instructions sent to that address.
5. Review the offer terms and complete enrollment.

After enrollment, sign in to the [Azure portal](https://portal.azure.com) with your personal Microsoft account. Your Azure for Students subscription should appear under **Subscriptions**.
